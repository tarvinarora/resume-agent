# User Confirmation Needed

These items could improve the resume further, but they should only be added if they are true. Answering these questions would help refine the resume toward a stronger estimated ATS match while staying honest.

## Digital Banking and Technology

1. Have you ever helped customers use a mobile app, online account, digital payment option, self-checkout, loyalty app, or other self-service technology?

2. If yes, what tool or platform did you help customers use, and what did you help them do?

3. Have you used or explained any banking, credit card, payment, or financial-services technology beyond POS systems and Mastercard product promotion?

4. Can you confidently discuss MS Power Apps in an interview? If yes, what did you use it for?

## Sales and Targets

1. What type of Mastercard product did you promote at SportChek? Was it a store credit card, rewards card, financial product, or another product?

2. Do you know any measurable sales results, such as number of Mastercard products sold, monthly targets exceeded, bonus frequency, ranking, or percentage above target?

3. Were you responsible for identifying simple sales opportunities based on customer needs?

4. Did you cross-sell, upsell, or recommend complementary products? If yes, what types of products?

## Customer Service and Relationship Building

1. About how many customers did you serve per shift or per week at SportChek?

2. About how many customers or transactions did you support per shift at BC Place Stadium?

3. Did you build repeat customer relationships or long-standing relationships with regular customers?

4. Do you have examples of explaining complicated product, payment, account, or policy concepts in simple language?

5. Do you have any customer compliments, recognition, feedback scores, or manager feedback that can be referenced honestly?

## Transactions, Cash, and Compliance

1. Did you handle cash, debit, credit, refunds, or bill payments directly at BC Place Stadium?

2. Did you balance tills, reconcile cash, close registers, prepare deposits, or review transaction reports?

3. Were you trained on fraud prevention, ID verification, responsible selling, privacy, AML, KYC, or any compliance process?

4. Did you resolve transaction errors, disputed charges, refund issues, or payment problems?

## Teamwork and Leadership

1. How many concession workers did you support or supervise at BC Place Stadium?

2. Did you train, coach, schedule, onboard, or guide any team members?

3. Did you collaborate with managers, operations staff, inventory teams, or other departments?

4. Were you recognized for teamwork, reliability, professionalism, or high performance?

## Availability and Role Fit

2. Are you legally eligible to work in Canada?
